# Unique Insights

- **Missing is a state machine, not a boolean.** Useful states include runtime-mounted, File-Library-located, device-local, exact-hash-only, functional-reconstruction, specification-reconstruction, scientific-state-reconstruction, and blocked-insufficient-source.
- **Reconstruction must preserve epistemic type.** A functional successor is not the historical original; a validation summary is not source code; a surviving hash is not recoverable bytes.
- **Fail-closed blockers are successful artifacts.** Refusing to fabricate a QNM result is preferable to a plausible but invented solver.
- **Durability must begin at artifact creation.** Immediate hashing, content-addressed storage, and repository/object-store persistence should be part of the generation transaction.
- **Execution and preservation should be decoupled.** Recovery packaging should reproduce and verify subordinate scripts without automatically executing installers, remote workers, or scientific workloads.
- **File Library and sandbox storage are distinct evidence layers.** Searchable account persistence does not imply a local filesystem path, and `/mnt/data` does not imply durable library persistence.
- **GitHub is the appropriate canonical Automation handoff.** A versioned branch/PR provides a durable, reviewable state transition with immutable commit identity.
