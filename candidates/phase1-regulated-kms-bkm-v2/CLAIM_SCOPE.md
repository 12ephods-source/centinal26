- Type II_infinity classification of the crossed product;
- Type II_1 classification of the regulated CLPW positive-energy corner;
- a continuum Connes Radon-Nikodym cocycle;
- emergent geometry;
- BKM equivalence to Hollands-Wald gravitational canonical energy;
- Gate 5 or Gate 6.

Gate 4 is a schema-v2 canonical definition, not a recovered historical definition.
