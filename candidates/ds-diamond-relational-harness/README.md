## Frozen geometry

In units `L_dS=1`, the 1+1 static-patch metric is

\[
ds^2=-(1-r^2)dt^2+(1-r^2)^{-1}dr^2.
\]

With tortoise coordinate

\[
r=\tanh x,
\]

it becomes

\[
ds^2=\operatorname{sech}^2x\,(-dt^2+dx^2).
\]

For a massless conformal scalar in two dimensions, \(\xi_2=0\), so after the conformal reduction the mode equation is the flat wave equation on the stretched interval

\[
x\in[-X,X],\qquad X=4,
\]

with Dirichlet boundary conditions at the stretched horizons.

The spatial operator is the standard second-difference discretization of \(-\partial_x^2\). With `N` interior sites,

\[
h=\frac{2X}{N+1},
\qquad
\omega_k^{(N)}=
\frac{2}{h}\sin\!\left(\frac{k\pi}{2(N+1)}\right),
\]

while the continuum Dirichlet frequencies are

\[
\omega_k=\frac{k\pi}{2X}.
\]

The scan uses `N={31,63,127,255}` and the first three modes. The harness independently diagonalizes the finite-difference matrix and checks its spectrum against the closed form.

\[

\[
